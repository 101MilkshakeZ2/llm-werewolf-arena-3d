Minecraft skins for the AI players (used when the top bar says "Bodies: Minecraft").
Put a skin here named after the player: Claude-Sonnet.png, Grok.png, Gemini-Lite.png ... (case, spaces and dashes are ignored).
A normal Minecraft skin: PNG, 64x64 (or the old 64x32). Classic and slim arms both work.
A player with no file here still gets a Minecraft body: a skin generated from their sprite (as the face) and their colour.
Your own skin (Upload skin button) always wins for you, whichever body style is picked.
